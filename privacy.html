Privacy Policy – BuildPlain Uploader

Last updated: 31 October 2026

BuildPlain Uploader (“the app”) is an internal publishing tool operated by Building Explained. Its only user is the owner of the Building Explained channel and accounts.

What data the app accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): the app uploads videos, titles, descriptions, tags and publish times to the owner’s own YouTube channel and reads basic channel information (channel ID and name) to confirm the correct channel.
  • Google Drive: the app creates folders and uploads video files into a folder in the owner’s own Drive.
  • Instagram / Meta: the app publishes the owner’s own videos (Reels) with captions to the owner’s own Instagram professional account and reads the account ID.

How data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the owner’s own content. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner’s computer and are never sold, shared with or transferred to third parties. The app does not collect data about viewers or any other users and does not use analytics or advertising.

Third-party services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy and the Meta Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings and in Instagram/Facebook settings under “Apps and websites”. Locally stored tokens are deleted on request or when access is revoked.
