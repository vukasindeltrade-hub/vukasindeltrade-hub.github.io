BuildPlain Uploader

BuildPlain Uploader is an internal tool used only by the Building Explained team. It uploads our own finished videos to our own YouTube channel (scheduled publishing), saves copies to our own Google Drive and publishes them to our own Instagram account. It is not offered to other users.
